Whereto

Every photo has somewhere to go. Keep what you want, delete what you don’t, and send what you need to the computer next to you.

Download on the App Store

Three ways to a tidy library

Sort

Swipe left to delete, right to keep

Photos appear one at a time so deciding is quick; swipe up to add one to an album. Sort at random or On This Day, or pick several months with the Full Version. Nothing is deleted until you confirm, and you can undo anytime.

Similar

Find duplicates in one scan

Six methods find duplicates, bursts, different versions of the same photo and possibly blurry shots, and suggest keeping the highest-resolution one. Compare two side by side with synced zoom.

Transfer

Same Wi-Fi, just a browser

Open the address on a computer or tablet, enter the pairing code, and download photos and files. No software, no cloud.

A closer look

What’s new in 1.3

  • Swipe up to add to an album: sort and organize in one go, even into several albums at once.
  • On This Day and several months at once: the free version can sort past years’ photos from today; the Full Version can pick several months together.
  • More on each card: Live Photo, burst, favorite and file size are shown, videos play right on the card, favorites ask once more before deletion, and photos in your Similar results show “N Similar”.
  • Summary grouped by month: multi-select to unmark, keep or delete in one go.
  • A better Similar scan: a new “Possibly Blurry” group and side-by-side compare with synced zoom; when you delete copies, their albums and favorites move to the photo you keep.
  • Full-screen view: Live Photos play, and videos have sound even in silent mode.
  • Transfer page: convert HEIC to JPG, and retry anything that didn’t download.
Your photos stay on your phone and your Wi-Fi. All analysis happens on your iPhone. Whereto has no servers of its own and needs no account.
