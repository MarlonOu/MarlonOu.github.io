Three places to go

Keep it, delete it, or send it on.

Whereto breaks tidying up into three small jobs. Each one happens on your iPhone, with no account and no cloud.

ASort

One look, one decision.

Photos appear one at a time. Swipe left to delete, right to keep, and up to drop it into an album as you go. Swiped the wrong way? One tap undoes it.

The name

Whereto: where to?

Whereto?

It’s the question every photo quietly asks. In Chinese the app is called 去處, “a place to go”, and there are three answers.

Keep→ your albums

Swipe right to keep it, or up to file it into an album.

Delete→ Recently Deleted · 30 days to recover

Deleted only when you confirm, with 30 days to change your mind.

Send→ the computer next to you

Same Wi-Fi, downloaded straight into a browser.
