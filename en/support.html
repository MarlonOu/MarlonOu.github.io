Sort

Is a photo deleted as soon as I swipe left?

No. Swiping left only marks it. Photos are deleted when you confirm in the trash at the top right, and iOS asks again. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and can be recovered within 30 days.

The free version only sorts 20 photos a day?

Yes — 20 a day, reset the next day. When you run out, you can watch an ad to sort 10 more (up to 3 times a day), or buy the Full Version for unlimited sorting. Undoing a swipe that puts the photo back gives that one back.

Similar

Could the similar scan delete different photos?

Only exact duplicates are selected automatically. Matches by appearance, bursts and AI are grouped but left for you to choose, and you confirm again before anything is deleted.

Scanning takes a long time with lots of photos

The first scan analyzes every photo; later scans only analyze new ones. Scanning can continue in the background, and you're notified when it's done.

Transfer

My computer can't open the address

Make sure the phone and computer are on the same Wi-Fi and the phone shows “Sharing on local network” in Transfer. The first time, iOS asks for Local Network access — choose Allow. If you declined, turn on Whereto in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Office or hotel Wi-Fi may block devices from connecting to each other; use your phone's Personal Hotspot instead.

The pairing code keeps failing or is locked

Five wrong tries from one device pauses it for 60 seconds; ten wrong tries across all devices changes the code on the phone. Always use the code currently shown on your phone.

Can I share only some albums?

Yes. In Transfer › Web Sharing › Choose Albums, turn off the albums you don't want to share. After “Turn All Off”, new albums aren't shared by default either.

Is transfer secure?

Only devices that enter the pairing code can see anything, and transfers never go over the internet. The connection itself isn't encrypted (HTTP), so use it on Wi-Fi you trust, like at home, and tap Stop when you're done.

Purchases

The Full Version is gone after I changed phones or reinstalled

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchase in Help › Full Version. You won't pay again.

Can my family use it?

Yes. The Full Version supports Family Sharing, so everyone in your family group can use it.

I'd like a refund

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

I saw an inappropriate ad

Go to Help › Full Version › Report an Inappropriate Ad. The email includes the ad's ID, and we'll report it to the ad network.
