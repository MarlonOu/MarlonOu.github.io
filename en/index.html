A closer look

What’s new in 1.2

  • Drag to select: in albums, folders and Similar, press and drag across photos to select many at once — just like the Photos app.
  • A better Similar scan: results are kept until your next scan, so you can pick up where you left off; swipe through a group in full screen and mark photos for deletion right there; see everything you’ve checked in one list.
  • Try AI for free: watch one ad to use “AI Visual Match” once and find the same scene shot from different angles.
  • iCloud download progress: when a photo is still in iCloud, the preview shows how far the download has got.
  • English, Japanese and iPad: the app follows your iPhone’s language, and the transfer web page follows your browser’s.
Your photos stay on your phone and your Wi-Fi. All analysis happens on your iPhone. Whereto has no servers of its own and needs no account.
