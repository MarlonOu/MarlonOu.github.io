我怎麼做 AppHow I build

三個原則Three rules

  1. 沒有我的伺服器No servers of mine

    照片、紀錄只在你的裝置與你自己的 iCloud。我拿不到，也不需要。Photos and records live on your device and in your own iCloud. I can’t see them, and I don’t need to.

  2. 打開就能用Open and go

    不用註冊、不用登入。要跟家人共用，就用你已經有的 Apple 帳號。No sign-up, no login. Sharing with family uses the Apple account you already have.

  3. 一次做好一件事One job, done well

    每個 App 只解決一個具體的麻煩：滿出來的相簿、忘了誰餵過的那一餐。Each app fixes one specific annoyance: an overflowing camera roll, the meal nobody remembers serving.
