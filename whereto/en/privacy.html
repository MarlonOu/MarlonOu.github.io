Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Whereto (“the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and where it's stored.

1. In short

  • Your photos and files are processed only on your iPhone. The app has no servers of its own and never uploads your photos anywhere.
  • The app doesn't require an account and doesn't collect your name, email or contact details.
  • Ads in the free version are provided by Google AdMob and are non-personalized. The app doesn't track your activity across other apps or websites.

2. Data the app accesses

Before deleting photos, iOS asks you to confirm. Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted and can be recovered within 30 days.

3. Local transfer

  • When Transfer is on, devices on the same Wi-Fi can reach the address shown on screen, but they must enter the 6-digit pairing code shown on your phone to see anything.
  • You choose which albums and folders to share, and you can disconnect all devices or turn off transfer anytime.
  • Transfers use HTTP (unencrypted), so we recommend using them only on Wi-Fi you trust, such as at home.

4. Ads (free version)

  • In the free version, a rewarded ad from Google AdMob plays only when you choose “Watch an Ad”. After you buy the Full Version, no ads are shown.
  • The app requests only non-personalized ads and doesn't ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).
  • To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device information, ad interactions, approximate location and diagnostics. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Users in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland see a consent screen first and can choose whether to consent.

5. Payments

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment information. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

The app isn't designed for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Retention and deletion

All data lives on your device. Deleting the app removes the files, marks, settings and analysis cache inside it; your photo library isn't affected.

8. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update this page and its effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, email quchu.app@gmail.com.
